---
name: Cohort role boundary
description: Why evidence owners cannot alone establish learner cohort membership
---

Count only authoritative learner-role users when enforcing cohort privacy.
Evidence record ownership alone is not proof of learner membership.

**Why:** Instructors can exercise learning features and create owned evidence.
Counting those records can turn four real learners into an apparently eligible
five-person cohort and bypass suppression.

**How to apply:** Resolve identities internally against authoritative roles before
unioning membership. Keep those identities and individual answers out of returned
aggregates. Test instructor participation and duplicate records at the threshold.

Whole-course membership is not sufficient to release a statistic. Apply distinct
contributor thresholds separately to gain, objectives, mastery competencies and
profile dimensions; modality counts and derived recommendations must not reveal
suppressed cells.

**Why:** Five course members can still contain only one person's pre/post
assessment or one answered profile dimension. A whole-cohort gate alone exposes
that person's exact statistic.
