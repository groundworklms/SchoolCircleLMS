One platform, two seats

What the Marine sees. What the instructor keeps.

Students get a clean path to mastery. Instructors stay in command of the course — the AI only ever hands them a draft.

The student

A clear path to mastery

  • ◆Ask the doctrine. A tutor that answers from the source with a citation — or an honest "not in the source."
  • ◆Adaptive study plan. Your week in three courses of action — behind, on track, or ahead — synced to the calendar.
  • ◆Discuss to mastery. A conversational check that scores each answer, coaches the gap, and records the score.
  • ◆Practice with rationale. Questions that tell you why an answer is right or wrong, at the difficulty you need.
  • ◆See your own mastery. A bar per topic against the question bank. Yours only.
The instructor

Command of the course

  • ◆Generate a course. A POI + the manual → cited lessons, tests, a scenario, and discussion prompts — left pending your review.
  • ◆Rubrics from standards. A T&R standard → a behaviorally-anchored rating scale, traceable, with reliability math.
  • ◆Run it live. A competitive in-class quiz off the generated bank — a live read on where the class actually is.
  • ◆Insight loop. Where the class missed and why, drafted into a short lesson review to get them back on track.
  • ◆Course AAR. End-of-course critiques, sorted into short- and long-term fixes — sharper every iteration.

Trigger control and follow-through minimize your arc of movement so the round leaves the muzzle while the sights are still on target. It takes a smooth, consistent rearward squeeze, finger where it naturally lays for the best mechanical advantage. The most common error is dropping control after the shot — keep your head on the stock, eye open, and hold the trigger to the rear through recoil until the sear resets.

How it stays honest

The same spine under every feature

Course, tutor, rubric, quiz — all of it runs the same four steps. That's why the guarantee holds everywhere.

01

Ground

Retrieve and re-rank the exact passages from the indexed corpus for the task at hand.

02

Cite or refuse

Answer only from those passages, with a citation — or abstain when they don't support one.

03

Verify

An on-device HHEM check scores faithfulness before anything is shown. Low score, no show.

04

Human review

The instructor approves, edits, or rejects. Nothing unreviewed reaches a Marine.

📡

Runs offline. Swaps models in one file.

The whole delivery loop runs on a Jetson Orin with the network pulled — built for the schoolhouse and the field. Every model call sits behind a single adapter, so if the compute at the event turns out to be something else, or shows up late, we change one file — not the app. There's a local path that works regardless of what allocation lands.
